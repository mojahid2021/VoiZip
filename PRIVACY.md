# VoiZip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

VoiZip is a phone and SIP (VoIP) calling app. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and why.

## Data that stays on your device

| Data | Why VoiZip uses it |
|---|---|
| **Microphone audio** | To carry your voice during a call. Audio goes only to the SIP provider you signed in with (and from there to the person you call). Call recordings, if you make any, are saved on your device only. |
| **Contacts** | To show names and photos for numbers, and to let you call or save contacts. |
| **Call log** | To show your SIM call history when VoiZip is your default phone app. |
| **Phone state and phone numbers** | To place and receive SIM calls and to pick the right SIM. |
| **SIP account details** (username, password, server) | To sign in to your SIP provider. Stored encrypted on the device and excluded from Android cloud backups. |

VoiZip does not upload your contacts, call log, phone numbers, recordings or SIP passwords to us or to anyone else.

## Data sent to third parties

| Data | Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Call audio, SIP signaling, the numbers or SIP addresses you call | **Your SIP provider** (chosen by you) | To make and receive calls. Their own privacy policy applies. |
| App usage events (for example "onboarding completed", "call connected", registration status), device model, Android version, app version, advertising ID | **Google Firebase Analytics** | To understand how the app is used and to fix problems. No phone numbers, contact names or call content are included. |
| Crash reports (stack traces, device model, Android version) | **Google Firebase Crashlytics** | To find and fix crashes. |
| Bug reports you choose to send: the text you type plus device diagnostics (app version, device model, Android version, network type, granted permissions) | **GitHub**, as a **public** issue | So we can investigate the problem. Do not type personal information into a bug report. |

Google's handling of Firebase data is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

All network traffic to these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). SIP traffic is encrypted when your provider supports TLS/SRTP.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not create accounts. You sign in with your own SIP provider's account, so there is no VoiZip account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device.

## Children

VoiZip is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this page when the app's data handling changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
